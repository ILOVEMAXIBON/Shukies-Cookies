PREVIEW
-------
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Keep index.html and the assets folder together.
3. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.

IMPORTANT: PAYMENT AND DELIVERY SETUP
-------------------------------------
The payment and delivery choices are currently preference/enquiry fields only.
This static website does NOT collect money, process card or PayPal payments,
submit orders to Uber Eats or DoorDash, calculate live delivery fees, or confirm
availability. No card numbers or security codes are collected by this page.

To accept card and PayPal payments directly on the website, connect a payment
provider and secure server-side checkout. PayPal's current checkout guides use
merchant/app credentials and a server-side create/capture flow. Keep all secret
keys off the public website. Do not ask customers to email card details.

To offer Uber Eats and DoorDash marketplace ordering, first create and activate
merchant listings, then replace the coming-soon labels with your exact public
storefront URLs. The delivery dropdown is a request preference until then.

Useful official setup pages:
Uber Eats Australia merchant signup:
https://merchants.ubereats.com/au/en/s/signup/
DoorDash Australia merchant information:
https://merchants.doordash.com/en-au
PayPal Checkout integration:
https://developer.paypal.com/checkout/integrate
PayPal Australia checkout options:
https://www.paypal.com/au/business/accept-payments/checkout/integration
